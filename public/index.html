Spot the tricks before you pay.

Paste a shopping link. We open the page like a careful shopper would and test it for fake timers, false scarcity and hidden fees.

What we found

    What we test

    Fake timers
    We note the countdown, refresh the page and check whether the clock starts over. A real deadline only goes down.
    False scarcity
    We look for "only 2 left" claims and see if the number holds up on a second visit. Results are flagged as likely, not proven.
    Hidden fees
    We compare the product price with the checkout total and stop before payment. Extra charges that appear late are flagged.

    Read-only audit. No purchase is made and no login is used.
